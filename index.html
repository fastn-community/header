What is header

A website header is the topmost section of a web page that appears above the main content and typically spans the entire width of the page. It often contains the website's logo, navigation menu, and sometimes other important information such as contact details or a search bar. The header is an important part of the website's design as it provides users with an immediate sense of the website's brand identity and helps them to navigate the site.
